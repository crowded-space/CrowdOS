Cross-cutting contract notes:
- Two parallel surfaces (bare fns + port object) are both public; aliases must not drift.
- All ops accept `db` override "to pin PG exactly", but the override is honored inconsistently: settings reads prefer local even with a db override (CHN-011), and create/update dup checks differ between db/non-db paths (CHN-013).
- Error style is mixed: unions for channel business failures, THROWS for category duplicate and missing workspaceId (CHN-006b/CHN-005), silent swallow for PG outages on reads/settings.
